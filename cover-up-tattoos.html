Cover-Ups That Erase the Past

Bio-organic depth over the tattoo you've outgrown.

Bio-organic is one of the strongest styles for covering old work. The deep values and organic flow can absorb almost anything underneath. If you have a tattoo you've outgrown, this is one of the best ways to replace it with something you're proud of.

A full guide — what's coverable, how I assess it, and before-and-after pairs — is on its way. Cover-up work is something I'm actively booking, and the fastest way to find out what's possible over your old piece is a free consultation. In the meantime, see the current cover-ups & reworks portfolio.

About

Carlos Barrera

“Elevating your story through ink.”

I'm a tattoo artist in love with my craft, and I've never stopped trying to get better at it. I've traveled for knowledge — to Japan, where a master told me to practice for a year before coming back (I did), and to artists across the country, including studying realism and portraiture under David Vega, regarded as one of the best black-and-grey realism artists in the world.

That's how I approach everything: study first, deeply, then make it my own. It's why I spent a year with books before my first bio-organic piece, and three years focused on floral before calling it one of my styles. When you sit in my chair, you're getting all of that — and my full attention on doing right by you.

My goal with every client is simple: that you trust me to create for you, and that trust is never misplaced.

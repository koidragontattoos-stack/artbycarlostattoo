Traveling for a Tattoo

Booking from out of state or abroad — how it works.

Clients travel to Murray, Utah from across the country and overseas to get tattooed by Carlos. Consultations happen remotely, the design is agreed before you book travel, and sessions are planned around your trip so nothing is left to chance.

A full guide to planning your visit — timing, sessions, healing before the flight home — is on its way. In the meantime, the fastest way to start is a free remote consultation.

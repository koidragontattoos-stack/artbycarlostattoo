The Tattoo Process

From first idea to healed piece — what working with Carlos looks like.

Every piece starts with a free consultation — in person at the studio in Murray, Utah, or remote if you're traveling. From there Carlos designs around your idea and your anatomy, and the session is booked once the direction feels right.

A full walkthrough of each step — consultation, design, session day and aftercare — is coming to this page. Until then, the FAQ on each style page answers the most common questions.
